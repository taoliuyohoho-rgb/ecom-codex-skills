# Nine-Image Pack Architecture

Use this reference before generating or materially revising a listing pack. Its purpose is to prevent nine cosmetically different product posters that say the same thing.

## Differentiation Matrix

Create one row per slot before production:

| Field | Required decision |
| --- | --- |
| Buyer question | One existing purchase question the image resolves |
| Persuasion role | `identity`, `factual proof`, `physical inspection`, `procedure`, `need-state`, `desired-state`, `routine/ritual`, or `verified decision support` |
| Evidence boundary | Exact fact source, approved claim, or `non_factual_lifestyle_copy` |
| Primary subject | Product, physical component, material, person/context, or care environment |
| Visual grammar | Packshot, material study, sequence, close detail, user-led lifestyle, or still-life ritual |
| Product visibility | Hero, supporting, partial bridge, or absent |
| Copy job | Commercial fact, instruction, approved claim, or non-factual lifestyle line |

Two slots may share the overall visual system, but they must differ in at least three of these fields. If they do not, merge their information jobs and reopen one slot for an unanswered buyer question.

## Useful Role Sequence

This is a planning menu, not a fixed template. Use only roles supported by the SKU evidence.

1. Identity — is this the exact product?
2. Formula or primary positioning — what is it made for or made with?
3. Verified format/count — what is included?
4. Texture or product form — what kind of product experience is this?
5. Physical component/detail — what should I inspect before buying?
6. How to use — what is the approved high-level sequence?
7. Need-state — when does this product category become relevant?
8. Desired-state — what everyday moment does the buyer want to return to?
9. Ritual/closing or verified decision support — how does it belong in a personal routine, or what verified uncertainty remains?

Do not force all nine roles. If the SKU lacks verified directions, compatibility, or a supportable claim, replace the slot only with a non-factual lifestyle/ritual role that does not imply the missing fact.

## Sensitive Personal-Care Guidance

- A need-state image names the friction. It should not show use or anatomy.
- A desired-state image shows the person moving into ordinary life. Product is normally a small visual bridge rather than the hero.
- A ritual/closing image shows a calm product environment, not a medical regimen, customer outcome, or second need-state scene.
- Keep these three roles distinct: friction, life moment, and personal routine. Do not repeat the same woman-in-room composition for each.

## Decision-Support Test

Use a decision-support image only if it resolves a buyer question that is both existing and source-verifiable. Reject a defensive question-and-answer treatment when it makes the viewer ask a problem they were not already considering, especially medicine, disease, safety, or treatment questions.

Without real review evidence, never imitate a review. An unquoted affirmative lifestyle thought is allowed when classified as `non_factual_lifestyle_copy`; it must not use stars, a username, date, testimonial framing, first-person outcome, or a claim that a product caused a result.

## Iterative User Feedback

When the human reviewer says “this is the same as the previous one,” replace the weak mission before regenerating. Do not solve a structural duplication problem with a new color, angle, headline, or prop alone.
